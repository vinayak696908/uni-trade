How UniTrade works

1

Create your student account

Sign up with your email and choose your campus. Your phone and email are never shown publicly.

2

List or find an item

Post an item with photos and a price in rupees, or filter by category, campus, condition and budget.

3

Chat and meet on campus

Send an inquiry through UniTrade, agree on a price and meet in a public place on campus.
